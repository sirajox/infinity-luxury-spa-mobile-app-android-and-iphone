# Admin guide (for the owner and staff)

Sign in at your dashboard address with the account your administrator created. What you see in the left menu depends on your role; if something is missing, your role does not include it.

## First-time setup (owner)

1. **Branches** → open each branch → set the real **opening hours** → tick **Opening hours confirmed** → tick **Accept online bookings**. Until then customers cannot book that branch.
2. **Staff** → add each therapist: photo, biography, **Works at branches**, **Treatments they perform**, then set the **Weekly schedule** (and breaks/leave).
3. **Treatments** → add prices and durations; choose the branches and staff. Set the buffer time if you need cleaning time between clients.
4. **Settings** → check booking rules, advance-payment percentage and cancellation windows. Enable the payment providers you have set up.
5. **Membership plans** → confirm price and benefits, then tick **Published** (they start unpublished).
6. **Policies & content** → paste your approved Privacy, Terms, Booking, Cancellation, Refund, Delivery and Membership text and tick **Published**.
7. **Delivery fees** → add the states you deliver to and the fee. Only these states can be chosen for home delivery.
8. **Products** → add products, then each size/price under **Sizes & prices**, then set opening stock under **Inventory → Adjust stock**.

## Day to day

- **Bookings**: filter by status/branch/date; open a booking to move it through *confirmed → checked in → in progress → completed*, mark a no-show, add notes, reschedule, or cancel. **Record payment at branch** for cash/POS. **New booking** books a walk-in (the customer needs an account).
- **Calendar**: one day, one branch, a column per specialist.
- **Orders**: update status, add tracking and an estimated date; the customer is notified. Payment confirmation is automatic.
- **Inventory**: adjust or transfer stock; a reason is required and recorded. Low-stock items turn red.
- **Payments / Refunds**: refunds queued by cancellations or duplicate payments appear under **Refunds**. **Process** sends the money back through the provider; **Decline** records why not. Export CSV from Payments or Reports.
- **Reviews**: approve genuine reviews to publish them.
- **Notifications**: send an announcement or offer. Offers reach only customers who opted in.
- **Support tickets**: reply to customer messages and close them when solved.
- **Maintenance mode** (Settings): show customers a "back soon" message while you work. Staff can still sign in.

## People and permissions

- **Users** → *Add staff* gives someone a role (optionally for one branch). **Roles & permissions** lets a Super Admin adjust what each role can do. Branch managers see only their branch.
- Never share logins. Remove a role when someone leaves (Users → Remove).
- **Audit logs** show who changed prices, settings, roles, stock, refunds and cancellations, and what they changed.

## Roles at a glance

Super Admin (everything) · Business Owner (everything except editing role definitions) · Branch Manager · Booking Manager · Store Manager · Inventory Manager · Finance Manager · Customer Support · Content Manager.

## Safety notes

- Prices, stock and totals are always recalculated by the system; you cannot accidentally charge a customer a wrong total by editing an app screen.
- Deleting is blocked when a record is in use; deactivate instead.
- Every sensitive action asks for confirmation.
